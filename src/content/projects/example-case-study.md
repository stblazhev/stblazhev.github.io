## Context

Set the scene in two or three short paragraphs. What is the product, who uses it, and what business problem made this research necessary? Keep company names and confidential numbers out; describe the company by type instead (for example "a multi-market online casino platform").

## The challenge

State the research question the team needed answered, and the decision that depended on it. A strong case study has one clear question here.

### Constraints

Time, budget, access to users, legal or compliance limits. Constraints show judgement: say how they shaped your choices.

## My role

What you personally did: planning, recruiting, moderating, synthesis, presenting. Name who you worked with.

## Approach

Explain each method and why it was the right one for the question, not just what it was.

| Phase | Method | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Competitor benchmark | Learn which patterns users already know |
| Evaluate | Unmoderated usability test | See where real users get stuck |
| Prioritise | Severity scoring with the team | Agree what to fix first |

## What we found

Group findings into a few insights. For each one: the insight in one sentence, the evidence, and why it matters.

### Insight 1: Users did not trust the form until they saw the brand

Evidence from the study goes here: success rates, observed behaviour, and quotes.
